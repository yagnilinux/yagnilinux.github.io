rag

rag is blackout's package manager, 251 lines of shell. Packages are bottles, they come in the crate, and your mix decides their fuel. yagnilinux/jits

rag [-r root] a|b|r|u|s|l|f [bottle]...

Installing

# rag a dev/git
# rag a git
# rag -r /mnt a dev/git
# RAG_REPO=file:///media/install/repo rag a dev/git

Finding

$ rag s
$ rag s fetch
$ rag l
$ grep -lx bin/oksh /var/db/rag/installed/*/*/manifest

Everything with sizes: packages.

Removing

# rag r utils/pfetch

Updating

# rag u

Fuel and the mix

To see what a bottle can be built with:

$ rag f qoltils
+wifi         wifi network password, for wpa2
+vol          vol 80, sets and unmutes the sound
+yt           yt url, plays audio or downloads with yt-dlp

Your mix is /etc/rag/mix:

*                 -doc
sys/qoltils       -yt
media/ffmpeg      =6.1-3
editors/scrawl    sha256:9f2c...
utils/pfetch      @latest

- and + take fuel out or put it in, = holds a version, sha256: checks the source, @latest builds from upstream. Then:

# rag u

To build one from source anyway:

# rag b scrawl

Your /etc

$ etcnew
$ etcnew diff /etc/profile
# etcnew take /etc/profile
# etcnew keep /etc/profile

/var/db/rag

/var/db/rag/repo/index                       the index
/var/db/rag/installed/<category>/<name>/   version, depends, manifest, etcsums, fuel
/var/db/rag/crate                            the bottles
/var/db/rag/cache                            downloads
/var/db/rag/stage                            unpacking
