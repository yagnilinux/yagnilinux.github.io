wifi

# jits a wpa_supplicant firmware-iwlwifi yutils
# wifi "network" "password"

this connects now and on every boot. intel cards need firmware-iwlwifi and realtek cards need firmware-realtek:

$ cat /sys/bus/pci/devices/*/vendor

0x8086 is intel and 0x10ec is realtek. only wpa2 is supported. usb tethering works if the phone is plugged in before boot.
