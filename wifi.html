wifi

“Do not spoil what you have by desiring what you have not.”
  — Epicurus

card package
Intel (iwlwifi) firmware-iwlwifi
Realtek (rtw88, rtw89) firmware-realtek

don't know your card? cat /sys/bus/pci/devices/*/vendor. 0x8086 is intel, 0x10ec is realtek. other cards need their firmware packaged.

setup

# jits a wpa_supplicant firmware-iwlwifi yutils
# wifi "network" "password"

connects now, and every boot after. networkmanager needs a daemon and dbus running to do the same.

no network at all

usb tethering and usb ethernet work. plug them in before you boot.

notes

wpa2 only. wpa3 and enterprise networks don't work.

the firmware is from march 2024. none of it has been tested on a real card yet.
