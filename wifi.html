-> wifi

out here you are on a cable unless you bring wifi yourself.

# cook a wpa_supplicant firmware-iwlwifi yutils
# wifi "network" "password"

that connects now and on every boot after. intel cards want firmware-iwlwifi, realtek cards want firmware-realtek. not sure what you are carrying:

$ cat /sys/bus/pci/devices/*/vendor

0x8086 is intel, 0x10ec is realtek. wpa2 only, wpa3 has not made it out here. usb tethering works if the phone is plugged in before boot.
