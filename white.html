let's not kid ourselves. the distribution you're running works. people use it. it ships a few gigabytes of things you will never open, and most days you don't notice. it's fine. it's also generic.

what i'm offering you is requiem.

  • every program is static. no loader, no /lib. it starts in 0.13ms. the same program on glibc takes 0.9.
  • the base system installs in under 3 seconds.
  • 100% gnu free. not almost. all of it.
  • the firmware starts the kernel, and the boot is 32 lines you can read in a minute.
  • purity: 99.1%.

so here's what we do. you keep your hardware. you install requiem. if something you need is missing, you package it and send it in, and i make sure it's up to standard before anyone else sees it.

or you go back to what you had. that's your choice. but think about what you'd be choosing.
