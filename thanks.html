“If I have seen further, it is by standing on the shoulders of giants.” — Isaac Newton

requiem is other people's ideas, stolen with love. these are the ones it owes the most.

sabotage
musl and busybox before anyone else. no /usr, no /sbin, and netbsd-curses. the missal is a cookbook with a different name.

kiss linux
packages as small build scripts, and a package manager you can read in one sitting.

dérive
a static musl and busybox desktop that is a whole desktop, not a demo.

openbsd
a base that is small, kept apart from the packages and written to be read. doas and ksh.

exherbo
breaking things when keeping them costs more, and not packaging the world from the centre.
